Installed

Rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Logic: it scans on launch; Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Reaper: Preferences > Plug-ins > Re-scan). Look under Renky Denk > Pipe-20 Phaser.

The second input bus (“CV In”) takes 1 V/oct and attenuverted control voltages from a DC-coupled interface. To retune the model after measuring hardware, place a tunables.json in ~/Library/Application Support/Pipe20Phaser/ and press Reload tunables in the plug-in.
