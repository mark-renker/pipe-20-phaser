Pipe-20 Phaser @VERSION@

An independent, non-commercial behavioural model inspired by the Tau “The Pipe” phaser and Jürgen Haible’s 20-stage redesign: a 20-stage all-pass phaser with 1 V/oct pitch control, resonance through self-oscillation, a triangle LFO, and complementary NORMAL / ALTERNATIVE outputs. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Random*Source or Tau Systems.

On the next screen tick the formats you want (Audio Unit, VST3, CLAP, standalone app). Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins; the app goes to /Applications. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW afterwards; it appears under Renky Denk > Pipe-20 Phaser.

Requires macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
